About Me

I am a seasoned professional in manufacturing systems technology with over a decade of experience across Garment Engineering, Data Analysis, Software Engineering, and Software Quality Assurance. My career is defined by a commitment to transforming complex manual manufacturing processes into streamlined, data-driven digital solutions through meticulous planning and structured development. My professional foundation began at PT Gistex Garment Indonesia (2013–2017) in Garment Engineering & Data Analysis, where I specialized in analyzing operational bottlenecks and designing robust system architectures. I then joined PT Shoetown Ligung Indonesia, progressing from Software Engineer Staff (2019–2023) to Software Engineer Specialist (2023–2025). In these roles, I focused on the full development lifecycle, building various Web and Android applications—such as Smart TPM, OEE Tracking, and Smart Mixing—while ensuring comprehensive technical documentation and system mapping for scalable implementation. My work is characterized by delivering significant operational impact, including developing tools that have reduced data processing time by up to 99% and minimized manual errors through automated system integrations. Since December 2025, I have transitioned into Software Quality Assurance (SQA). Leveraging my extensive background as both an engineer and an analyst, I now focus on ensuring the reliability and performance of enterprise-scale applications, such as the Apollo Management System, through detailed functional testing, Katalon automation, and JMeter performance analysis to support up to 1,500 concurrent users.
